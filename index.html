How feedback and revision work

Complete · 2

Meaningfully addresses the component with relevant explanation and rationale.

Revise · 1

Addresses the component but needs further investigation, explanation, or reconsideration.

Incomplete · 0

Missing or does not meaningfully address the task.

Each CCC component receives 0–2 points (6 total). An initial score below 6/6 requires revision of all components that did not earn 2 points. Faculty rescore the revised work.

Final score: 5–6 earns full assignment credit; 4 earns 80%; 0–3 requires additional remediation (0 credit if remediation is not successfully completed), subject to your instructor’s course policy.

This feedback protocol evaluates the written learning activity, not independent clinical reasoning or competency. Submit the exported file through your institution's LMS as directed.
